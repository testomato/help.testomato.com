Testomato accepts [PayPal](https://paypal.com/) in addition to Visa, Mastercard, and Discover. You can change your payment method to PayPal at any time through the Testomato website at [Your profile > Billing > Billing details](https://testomato.com/user/payments/billing/details).

## Pay with PayPal

Choose **PayPal** from **Payment method**:

![Screenshot showing payment selection UI](/img/payment/payment-methods.png)

Click the PayPal checkout button and follow PayPal instructions.

![Screenshot showing Paypal checkout button](/img/payment/paypal.png)
